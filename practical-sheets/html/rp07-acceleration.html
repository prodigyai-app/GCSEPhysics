Method: Part A — varying the force

  1. 1
    Choose Vary the force. Tick Runway tilted to compensate for friction.
  2. 2
    Set Masses on the hanger to 1. Press Release the trolley. Copy the times at gate 1 (t₁), gate 2 (t₂) and between the gates (t₁₂).
    The other masses ride on the trolley, so the total mass stays 0.600 kg.
  3. 3
    Repeat for 2, 3, 4 and 5 masses on the hanger.
  4. 4
    Calculate v₁ = 0.050 ÷ t₁, v₂ = 0.050 ÷ t₂ and a = (v₂ − v₁) ÷ t₁₂.
    The card on the trolley is 5.0 cm (0.050 m) long.

Analysis

First draw your graphs on the last page. On Graph A, plot acceleration (y-axis) against force (x-axis) from Table 1. Draw a line of best fit. On Graph B, plot acceleration (y-axis) against 1 ÷ mass (x-axis) from Table 2. Draw a line of best fit.

Conclusion and evaluation

1

Describe the relationship between acceleration and force shown by Graph A.

2

Describe how the acceleration changed as the mass increased.

3

Why was the runway tilted slightly?

4

In Part A, why were the masses moved from the trolley to the hanger, rather than new masses added?
