Analysis

First draw your graph on the last page. Plot temperature (y-axis) against energy transferred (x-axis) from Table 1. Draw a line of best fit through the straight part of the graph.

1

Find the gradient of your line: gradient = change in temperature ÷ change in energy. Show the triangle on your graph.

2

Calculate the specific heat capacity: c = 1 ÷ (gradient × mass). The mass is 1.00 kg.

3

The accepted value for aluminium is 900 J/kg °C. Calculate the percentage difference: (your value − 900) ÷ 900 × 100.

Conclusion and evaluation

1

Is your value higher or lower than 900 J/kg °C? Explain why.

2

Use Table 2. How did the jacket and oil change the temperature rise? Explain why.

3

Why do you use only the straight part of the graph to find the gradient?

4

Suggest one way to make the real experiment more accurate.
