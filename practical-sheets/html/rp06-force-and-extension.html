Analysis

First draw your graph on the last page. Plot force (y-axis) against extension (x-axis) for both springs, with a key. Draw a line of best fit for each.

1

Calculate the spring constant of spring A: k = force ÷ extension. Use a point on the straight part and convert the extension to metres.

2

Calculate the elastic potential energy stored in spring A at an extension of your choice: Eₑ = ½ k e².

Conclusion and evaluation

1

Describe your graph for spring A. Does it show that force is directly proportional to extension? Explain.

2

Which spring is stiffer? How can you tell from the graph?

3
